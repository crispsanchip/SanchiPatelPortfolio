The Impractical Catculator

The Impractical Catculator is a hackathon project made for SunHacks 2024. It was made by Ryan Schoenthal, Jacob Kaufman-Warner, Sanchi Patel, and Daniel Ortiz. This won the Most Impractical and Best Mascot categories.
We found inspiration from the "Most Impractical" award. One of our friends came up with the idea of a calculator that doesn't function properly and makes calculating anything annoying for its users. We loved that idea, so we made it!
Have you ever wanted to convert numbers into dates? How about converting numbers to emojis? The Impractical Catculator has everything you would never need (and nothing you actually need)!
I was in charge of programming some of the functionality within the project, mainly front end HTML/CSS that affected the appearance. I was also in charge of the design and aesthetics of the project, the user interface, and all art assets.

Medeival Music Mayhem
This was a project I worked on as a 2D artist for Mid-Evil Studios. This was used as the final project for CPI111.
In Medieval Music Mayhem, you become the Music Wizard, dueling magical foes in rhythm-based battles to save the kingdom from the sinister Wizard Beathalzar, who has overthrown the king. Along the way, you’ll face unique enemies like the Captain of the Knights, testing your timing and strategy against their unpredictable, lightning-charged, and explosive special arrows.
With each song, the rhythm intensifies, pushing your reflexes and precision to the limit. Victory brings you closer to unraveling the kingdom’s secrets and confronting Beathalzar in an epic showdown. After each duel, you’ll receive a performance score, allowing you to refine your skills or aim for the highest score. Every battle is a step closer to saving the kingdom with the power of music!
I worked mainly on the background illustrations, the home screen art, and some of the enemy designs.
